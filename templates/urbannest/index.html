FAQ

Questions parents usually ask

What is the admission process?

Book a visit on WhatsApp, tour the hostel, choose your room and reserve it with a one-month refundable security deposit. Aadhaar and one passport photo are required at check-in.

What are the entry and exit timings?

The gate closes at 10:30 PM. Late entry for classes or tests is allowed with prior notice to the warden. Parents receive an SMS on every biometric entry and exit.

Is there a notice period for leaving?

Yes — one month's written notice. The security deposit is refunded in full within 7 days of checkout after room inspection.

Can parents visit and stay?

Parents are welcome every day between 8 AM and 8 PM. We also keep two guest rooms that parents can book for overnight stays at nominal charges.

What happens if a student falls ill?

A doctor visits on call within 30 minutes, and our tie-up hospital is 2 km away. The warden informs parents immediately and arranges special mess food during recovery.
