Safety, By Design

Six layers of real security

Not promises — systems. This is exactly how we keep 120 residents safe, every single day.

Layer 01

Biometric entry

Fingerprint access at the main gate. Parents receive an instant SMS on every entry and exit.

Layer 02

All-women staff

Security guards, wardens and housekeeping inside the residence are all women, on duty round the clock.

Layer 03

CCTV coverage

42 cameras cover every corridor, entrance and common area — monitored live, recorded for 60 days.

Layer 04

Verified visitors

Every visitor is ID-verified and logged. Male visitors meet residents only in the ground-floor lounge.

Layer 05

Emergency response

Panic buttons on each floor, a nurse on campus and a tie-up hospital 1.5 km away with 24×7 ambulance.

Layer 06

Safe commute

Verified auto and cab partners for late returns, and a warden escort from the gate after 9 PM.

A Day at Vedika

Gentle routines, good days

Life here has a rhythm — structured enough to feel safe, free enough to feel yours.

6:30 AM

Yoga on the terrace

Optional morning yoga and meditation with instructor Meera, three days a week as the city wakes up.

8:00 AM

Breakfast, fresh and hot

Poha, idli, parathas, fruit and filter coffee — a rotating menu from our all-women kitchen team.

9:00 AM – 7:00 PM

College, office, your day

Verified commute partners at the gate, packed lunch available on request, and the residence quietly reset while you're away.

8:00 PM

Dinner together

The dining hall fills up — dal, sabzi, phulkas and one comfort dish daily. Sunday is everyone's favourite: chhole bhature night.

10:30 PM

Doors close, campus settles

Biometric entry closes for the night (late passes available for work), the night warden begins rounds, and the building goes quiet.
