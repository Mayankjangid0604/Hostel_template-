The Idea

We removed everything that breaks concentration.

No television lounge on the study floors. No corridor noise after 9 PM. No unreliable Wi-Fi that turns a physics doubt into a two-hour detour. Every decision in this building was made by asking one question: does it help a student sit and study for four uninterrupted hours?

What remained is quiet, structure, good food and adults who notice when a student goes quiet in the wrong way.

The building doesn't help you study. It just stops getting in the way.
Rohit Agarwal · AIR 842, JEE Advanced 2025
