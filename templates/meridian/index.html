The House

A century of hospitality, restored by hand

Built in 1923 for a Dutch spice merchant, Meridian House spent decades as a family home before three years of careful restoration returned its teak beams, athangudi tiles and lime-plastered walls to their original quiet grandeur.

Today it is an eleven-room hotel run the old way: the kitchen knows your coffee order by the second morning, the staff outnumber the rooms, and nothing — not check-in, not dinner, not the day itself — is ever hurried.
